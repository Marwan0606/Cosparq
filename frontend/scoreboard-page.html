Score Bands

  • 85–100 Exceptional — top-tier in its category.
  • 70–84 Strong — clear strengths with minor gaps.
  • 50–69 Average — solid foundation, notable weaknesses.
  • 30–49 Below Average — significant concerns.
  • 0–29 Poor — fundamental issues, needs full rethink.

Criteria

  • Originality — How novel is the idea? Are there direct competitors or is this a new category?
  • Market Demand — Is there real, validated demand? Considers TAM size and pain point urgency.
  • Technical Feasibility — How hard is it to build? Evaluates tech stack complexity, timeline, and risk.
  • Barrier to Entry — How defensible is the business? Looks at moats, network effects, and IP.
  • Monetization Potential — Can this make money? Assesses revenue models and willingness to pay.
  • Scalability — Can it grow efficiently? Considers marginal costs and growth loops.
  • Regulatory & Legal — How complex is compliance? Higher score means fewer hurdles.

Overall Score

The Overall score is not a simple average. It's weighted by real-world startup success factors:

  • ~40% Market Demand + Monetization
  • ~30% Technical Feasibility + Barrier to Entry
  • ~20% Originality + Scalability
  • ~10% Regulatory & Legal Complexity
